\chapter{Discussion}
\label{chap:discussion}

This chapter explains what the results mean and how far the evidence supports your conclusions. Introduce your main interpretation and flag the limitations that matter most before going into detail.

\section{Interpretation}
Start with one paragraph answering the research question in light of the results. Use the next few paragraphs to compare the answer with prior work and consider plausible alternative explanations. Calibrate the strength of each claim to the evidence your study can provide.

\section{Limitations}
Usually a few focused paragraphs are enough. For each important limitation, say which finding or interpretation it affects and how. For example, explain who may be underrepresented by the sample and where that makes it harder to apply the findings.
