\chapter{Discussion}
\label{chap:discussion}

\section{Interpretation}
Start with one paragraph answering the research question in light of the
results. Use the next few paragraphs to compare the answer with prior work
and consider plausible alternative explanations. Calibrate the strength of
each claim to the evidence your study can provide.

\section{Limitations}
Usually a few focused paragraphs are enough. For each important limitation,
say which finding or interpretation it affects and how. For example, explain
who may be underrepresented by the sample and where that makes it harder
to apply the findings.
