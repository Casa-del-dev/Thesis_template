\chapter{Methods}
\label{chap:methods}

Explain how your design answers the research question. Give readers enough
detail to evaluate and, where applicable, reproduce your approach.

\section{Data and analysis}
Describe where the evidence comes from and outline how you analyzed it.

\subsection{Data preparation}
Explain how you prepared the data, including any exclusions or transformations
that affect the results.

\subsubsection{Missing observations}
If relevant, explain how you handled missing observations and why you chose
that approach.

% Keep this section if your thesis includes a user study.
\section{User study}
Describe the participants, tasks, procedure, and measures used to evaluate
the work. Report any relevant consent and ethics procedures.
