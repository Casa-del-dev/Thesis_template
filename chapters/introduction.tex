\chapter{Introduction}
\label{chap:introduction}

In about 1--2 pages, take the reader from the broader problem to the specific
gap your study addresses. Explain why the gap matters and who would benefit
from an answer. The reader should reach the research question with enough
context to understand both its motivation and its scope.

\section{Research question}
A short paragraph is usually enough. State the main question explicitly and
define the population, setting, or system it concerns. Include secondary
questions only when the study can answer them with its evidence.

\section{Contribution}
In one paragraph or a short list, say what the thesis contributes and what
evidence supports it. For a system thesis, distinguish what you built from
what you learned by building or evaluating it.
