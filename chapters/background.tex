\chapter{Related Work}
\label{chap:background}

Start by naming the research area and the strands of prior work that
readers need to understand your study. The section below shows how to
review those strands and explain where your contribution fits.

\section{Relevant literature}
Start with 1--3 pages organized around the ideas, methods, or findings
that matter to your research question. Compare studies where their results
or assumptions differ, then identify the gap your work addresses. Each
paragraph should help the reader understand why your question or approach
follows from the literature.
